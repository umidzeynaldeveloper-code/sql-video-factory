🕛 An Oracle DATE always has a time
   → DATE '2026-11-30' = midnight
✅ Half-open range
   → from Nov 1 inclusive, up to Dec 1 exclusive
   → index friendly, no TRUNC on the column
⏱️ TIMESTAMP: fractions up to 9 digits (default 6)
➗ DATE − DATE = days (0.5 = 12 h)
   TS − TS = INTERVAL DAY TO SECOND
🌍 SYSDATE = DB server clock · CURRENT_DATE = session time zone
   → TIMESTAMP WITH LOCAL TIME ZONE for global apps
📆 ADD_MONTHS(Jan 31, 1) → Feb 28
   → + INTERVAL '1' MONTH → ORA-01839
🚫 TO_DATE without a format depends on NLS settings
