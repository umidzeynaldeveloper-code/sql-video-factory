You open the execution plan… 📜
6 lines, weird indentation, where do you even START? 😵
Hint: NOT at the top ☝️

🎯 Get the REAL plan:
   → /*+ GATHER_PLAN_STATISTICS */
   → DBMS_XPLAN.DISPLAY_CURSOR with format 'ALLSTATS LAST'
🌳 Read it as a TREE: deepest first child runs first
🌊 Rows flow UP: hash table from branches ← probes from nested loops
🔎 #1 trick: E-Rows vs A-Rows
   → expected 10, got 1,000,000 = wrong plan 💥
🔁 Starts: how many times a step ran (nested loops inner = once per row!)
🔥 Buffers + A-Time: the biggest line is your hotspot
⚖️ Predicates:
   → access = used to FIND rows ✅
   → filter = read, then thrown away ❌
📝 Note section: baselines, dynamic statistics

Don't guess. The plan tells you exactly where the time went 🕵️
