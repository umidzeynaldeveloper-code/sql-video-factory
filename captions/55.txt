✏️ Every change is written in pencil
🖊️ COMMIT = rewrite in pen: permanent, everyone sees it
🧽 ROLLBACK = eraser: back to the last commit
📦 Transaction = all changes, or none
🙈 Before COMMIT only YOU see your changes
💥 Crash? Oracle rolls back automatically
⚠️ DDL (CREATE, ALTER, DROP, TRUNCATE)
   → secret COMMIT before and after
🔒 Uncommitted rows stay locked
   → colleagues wait until you finish

Always finish what you start: COMMIT or ROLLBACK 💡
