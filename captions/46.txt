5 workers. 1 queue. The same job paid 5 times 💸
Or everyone waits all night ⏳

🔒 FOR UPDATE locks the selected rows until COMMIT / ROLLBACK
   → plain SELECTs still read them
⏱️ How long to wait?
   → default: forever
   → NOWAIT → ORA-00054 resource busy
   → WAIT 3 → ORA-30006 after 3 seconds
🚀 SKIP LOCKED: rows locked by others are skipped
   → every worker gets its own jobs, nobody waits
🧑‍💻 Cursor … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED + BULK COLLECT LIMIT 10
⚠️ ROWNUM 10 + SKIP LOCKED can return 0 rows
   → ROWNUM picks rows BEFORE locked ones are skipped
🎯 FOR UPDATE OF j.status · WHERE CURRENT OF c
🚫 COMMIT inside a FOR UPDATE loop → ORA-01002

Lock what you process, skip what others hold 💡
