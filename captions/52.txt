SELECT city, amount … GROUP BY city
→ ORA-00979: not a GROUP BY expression 😵

🧾 GROUP BY = sort receipts into piles by city
   → 7 rows become 3 piles
🧮 Aggregates turn a pile into ONE value
   → COUNT(*) · SUM · AVG · MIN · MAX
❓ One line per pile, but Baku has 3 amounts
   → which one goes in the cell? Oracle refuses
📏 The rule: every column in SELECT is
   → in GROUP BY, or inside an aggregate
🛠️ Fix 1: MAX(amount) · Fix 2: GROUP BY city, amount
👥 GROUP BY city, year → smaller piles
🕳️ COUNT(*) counts rows · COUNT(coupon) skips NULLs

Ask: what ONE value do I want per pile? 💡
